About

I work with Excel, SQL, Power BI, and data visualization to explore data, uncover patterns, build interactive dashboards, and turn numbers into insights that people can actually use. My journey into data has been driven by curiosity and a desire to solve real-world problems. From analyzing datasets to building business intelligence projects, I’m constantly learning, experimenting, and finding better ways to communicate what the data is saying. This portfolio is a collection of my journey so far, the projects I’ve built, the skills I’ve developed, and the problems I’ve explored through data. I believe good data analysis is not just about numbers. It’s about asking the right questions, finding the story, and making the insights useful.
